01 The human bit

I am happiest when I am making computers do my bidding. Politely, most of the time.

I am a full-stack developer based in Walton-on-Thames. My route here was a little unconventional, with stops in journalism, a brief detour into civil engineering, and several years in localisation, content, and design before I finally stopped pretending technology wasn't the destination all along.

I'm currently waiting for results of a BSc in Computing while working in localisation, which means I spend my days translating things. Sometimes it's English to Portuguese. Sometimes it's technical jargon into something a normal human would actually understand. Both come in handy when building software.

I enjoy building apps that solve real problems, whether that's helping football admins manage oversubscribed matches or creating something simply because I wanted to know if I could. I like thoughtful design, code that make it work, and asking "why?" almost as often as "how?".

Outside of coding, you'll usually find me cooking something overly ambitious, watching football, getting lost in a great TV series, or confidently telling my family, "This should only take five minutes," moments before discovering I've just volunteered my entire evening.
