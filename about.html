01 The human bit

I am happiest when I am making computers do my bidding. Politely, most of the time.

I am a full-stack developer based in Walton-on-Thames who took a few successful detours through localization, content management, graphic design, journalism, and civil engineering before finally admitting that technology was where I wanted to be.

I am currently pursuing a BSc in Computing while working as a Localization Specialist. My background means I can translate between technical jargon and human language almost as well as I translate between Portuguese and English.

I care about clean, useful experiences and code that does not make the next developer consider a completely different career. I bring analytical thinking, stakeholder experience, design sense, and a healthy willingness to ask why.

Away from the screen, I cook elaborate meals, watch football, play games, binge an unreasonable number of series, and tell my family I only need five more minutes to fix one tiny bug.
